From Tokyo to Fuji and Hakone

Travel comfortably from Tokyo with a private sightseeing taxi.

Mount Fuji is always near the top of the itinerary for many travelers. However, combining it with the hot spring town of Hakone on your own—navigating trains, local buses, a ropeway, and a sightseeing boat all in one day—is no easy task. Packing everything into a seamless single-day route removes all the hassle of planning. Since you have a private sightseeing taxi for the entire day, you can choose to end your trip back in Tokyo or head straight to your next destination, Kamakura.

01

Mt. Fuji’s 5th Station

The tour bus climbs from Tokyo to Mt. Fuji’s 5th Station, 2,300 metres above sea level. The air here is noticeably thinner and cooler; a small shrine sits partway up the mountainside, and the surrounding lakes appear far below, laid out like a map.

02

Lake Ashi by boat

From Fuji, the route descends into Hakone’s volcanic valley for a crossing of Lake Ashi aboard a wooden-hulled sightseeing boat. The vermillion torii gate of Hakone Shrine stands in the water near the far shore, framed by forested slopes — one of the more photographed, and more genuinely quiet, views in the region.

03

Above the valley on the Hakone Ropeway

The Hakone Ropeway lifts the tour above the valley for a wide view of its volcanic terrain. On a clear day, Mt. Fuji reappears here from an entirely different angle, standing over the ridgeline.

04

The ride home by Shinkansen

At the end of the day, you can relax in the comfort of your vehicle as you head back to Tokyo, or extend your trip with an overnight stay in Kamakura—the choice is completely yours.
