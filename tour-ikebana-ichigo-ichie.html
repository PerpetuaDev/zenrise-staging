Experience the Art of Ikebana in Kamakura with Master Koen Yokoi

Immerse yourself in "Ichika Ichiei" (One Flower, One Encounter)—a unique, 90-minute private floral workshop designed for exclusive small groups of 1 to 6 guests in the historic city of Kamakura.

Guided by Master Koen Yokoi, a third-generation Sogetsu Ikebana master with an illustrious career decorating stage productions, international events, and film sets, this private session offers a rare and intimate glimpse into the world of Japanese flower arrangement.
