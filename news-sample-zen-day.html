New Tour: A Morning of Zen in Kita-Kamakura

Zazen, temple gardens, and a bowl of matcha — autumn bookings now open

Kamakura is at its best before the day-trippers arrive. Our new half-day tour starts early for exactly that reason — you sit, walk, and drink tea while the temples still belong to the morning.

01

Zazen before the city wakes

The morning begins in the meditation hall of a working temple in Kita-Kamakura, where a monk guides a short session of zazen — seated meditation. No experience is needed, and instructions are given in English.

The session lasts about forty minutes, with a short talk afterwards over the temple's own roasted tea.

02

Gardens in the quiet hours

From the hall we walk the temple grounds and the lanes of Kita-Kamakura while they are still empty. Your guide reads the gardens as you go — why the moss is left to spread, what the stone arrangements are doing, and how the view is composed from the veranda.

03

A bowl of matcha to finish

The morning ends in a tatami room overlooking the garden, where matcha is whisked in front of you and served with a seasonal sweet. It is a small ceremony rather than a formal one — a chance to sit with what the morning gave you before the town fills up.

Dates and booking

The tour runs Tuesday to Sunday mornings from late September through November, starting at 7:00 from Kita-Kamakura Station. It finishes around noon, leaving the afternoon free for the rest of Kamakura.

Booking

Autumn sessions are limited to eight guests per morning. Dates and availability are listed on Viator.
