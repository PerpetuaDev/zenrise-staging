# Zen Journey page — repetition and terms-overlap audit

Audited 2026-08-27 against `terms.html`. Everything below is Bokun-sourced copy,
so the fixes are the client's to make in the panel — Bokun stays the single
source and the site rebuilds hourly. The one site-side change is done: the
Other info block now links to the full terms.

Line numbers are the Other info items in render order: **Included 1–9**,
**Not included 10–13**, **What to bring 14**, **Good to know 15–22**.

## C. Repeated inside the page

1. **Stone steps / footwear — three times.** Lede paragraph 5; What to bring 14;
   Good to know 15. Line 14 also says it twice within itself: "Comfortable
   walking shoes. There are temples with stone steps, so we recommend
   comfortable shoes." Keep one — What to bring is the natural home.
2. **Insurance — three times.** Included 7 (eco-tour insurance is included), Not
   included 13 (your own overseas cover is not), Good to know 19 (restates both).
   Keep 7 and 13, which are genuinely "what you get / don't get"; 19 goes to terms.
3. **Meeting point and hotel transfers — three times.** Included 9 (meet at
   Kita-Kamakura), Not included 11 (transfers quoted separately), Good to know 16
   (both again). Line 16 is the only one that says where to meet in a sentence, so
   it may be the keeper — but then 9 and 11 are redundant with it.
4. **Lunch preferences / dietary — twice.** Included 5 and Good to know 17.
   Line 17 adds the "tell us at booking" instruction and the seasonal nagashi
   somen note, so it is the fuller one.
5. **What's included — three layers.** Lede paragraph 5 summarises it
   ("travel expenses, admission fees, lunch, accompanying a guide"), the chips
   list it (Bus fare, Parking fees, Food & drinks, Entry & admission fees, Tax),
   and Included 1/2/5/6 spell it out again. The chips and the prose are the same
   Bokun fields by design; the lede sentence is the one that could go.
6. **Itinerary — three times.** Zazen at Engaku-ji appears in lede paragraph 3,
   Included 3, and route stop 01. Matcha at Jomyo-ji in lede paragraph 3,
   Included 4, and route stop 03. Lunch in lede paragraph 3, Included 5, and
   route stop 02. The route is the place for sequence; Included should say what
   is paid for, not re-narrate the day.

## D. Copy defects worth fixing while in there

- **Line 7** has an unclosed bracket: "(including domestic travel accident
  insurance and facility liability insurance. (International customers are also

  lunch of choice".
- **Line 14** is internally redundant (see C1).

## Net effect if A and B are actioned

Good to know drops from 8 lines to 3 or 4, and the four terms-grade paragraphs —
the longest in the block — leave the page entirely. Nothing is lost to the
customer: the Other info section now ends with a link to the full terms.
