Tour No. 05

Yokohama Harbour, After Dark

Noge, early evening © Zenrise
Tour
No. 05
Area
Yokohama
Format
Half-day
Length
4 hours
Group

Yokohama changes character once the light goes. This walk begins where the harbour opens out, follows the water while the cranes are still lit, then turns inland into Noge — a few streets of small counters and older bars that have outlasted everything built around them. We keep the pace slow and the group small, and finish somewhere you would not have found on your own.

Included
  • English-speaking guide
  • One drink at the final stop
  • Harbour-side walk and Noge back lanes
