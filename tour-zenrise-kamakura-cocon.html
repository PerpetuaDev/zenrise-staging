An extraordinary fusion of luxury stay, private tour, and fine dining.

Experience a seamless journey of elegance and tranquility in Kamakura through a tailored private tour and exquisite culinary art.

Nestled in the quiet Yato valley of Nikaido, "Kamakura Cocon" is an exclusive all-suite auberge hosting only two groups per day, lovingly restored from a historic residence built in 1855 (Ansei 2). Surrounded by stone-paved pathways, lush moss gardens, and seasonal views through lattice windows, this sanctuary promises a peaceful escape for discerning guests.

Experience Highlights

Custom Private Guided Tour: A knowledgeable local guide designs a personalized itinerary tailored to your pace and preferences, introducing you to Kamakura’s hidden gems, history, and rich culture. Select either a Half-day (4-hour) or Full-day (8-hour) tour option.

Exquisite Dining at "Restaurant COCON": Enjoy a multi-course ぅlunch or dinner (7 courses & 2 desserts) seated at the open kitchen counter. Savor dishes crafted with Italian culinary techniques using carefully selected seasonal ingredients from Kamakura, Shonan, and across Japan.

Luxury Accommodation (When choosing a stay plan): Unwind in spacious, historic suite rooms (Maisonette Suite 101 or Kura Suite 102) equipped with micro-bubble baths and airweave mattresses for ultimate rejuvenation. For overnight stays, please book via the accommodation plan page.

Important Notes

Closed Days: Restaurant COCON is closed on Wednesdays and Thursdays.

Dietary Restrictions & Allergies: Due to the nature of our ingredients, we are unable to accommodate allergies to flour, onions, or garlic. Please inform us of any other dietary requirements or allergies when booking.

Group Size: Standard bookings accommodate up to 6 guests (please contact us in advance if you plan a stay for 3 or more guests).
