Kamakura's temples have been protected for 850 years. That serenity has also been around the same number of years. This tour is a day to enjoy that serenity. In addition to visiting famous places, you will also slowly visit the three Zen temples of the Kamakura Five Mountains, and the guide will carefully guide you through the scenery in front of you one by one. The Kamakura Gozan is a general term for the five highest ranking temples of the Rinzai sect — Kenchoji, Engaku-ji, Jufuku-ji, Jochi-ji, and Jomyo-ji — founded by the Kamakura Shogunate and determined by the Muromachi Shogunate. At Engakuji, you will experience a short zazen meditation; at Jochi-ji Temple, you will walk around the precincts decorated with seasonal flowers, and enjoy matcha while looking at dry mountain water at Jomyo-ji Temple. At the end of the morning, have lunch at a restaurant that suits your preferences. All arrangements have been made in advance, so all that's left to do is be prepared to walk slowly. Practical Notes There are temples with stone steps, so we recommend shoes that are easy to walk in. We will guide you on an all-inclusive basis, including travel expenses, admission fees, lunch, and accompanying a guide.
