Kamakura in the Rain: Five Quiet Places for June

The rainy season empties the lanes — and the temples are at their greenest

Most visitors plan around the rain. We plan for it — some of our favourite mornings of the year happen under an umbrella. Here are five places that reward a wet June day.

01

Moss, stone, and rain

Rain is what the moss gardens are for. The stones darken, the green saturates, and the gravel courts hold the sound of falling water. Temples that feel austere in August feel alive in June.

02

Walking it well

Wear shoes you trust on wet stone, carry a small towel the way locals do, and start early — even in June the afternoon brings tour groups. Every place on this list is within fifteen minutes of a station, so a sudden downpour is never a problem.

Join us in the rain

Our June departures run rain or shine, with umbrellas provided. The gardens are better for it — we promise.
